Count definitions

New excludes public-adopted spectroscopic M0+ objects (including confirmed duplicates) and both claim lists. Confirmed: moving [5–6], independently of spectral-quality vetting. Candidate: good spectrum and maybe moving [3–4], or good_candidate spectrum without a negative motion decision, including objects not yet motion-vetted. Types use the latest SUBLIME2 autotype; T0+ is included in L0+. Classified means any WiseView decision. Rejected means a negative WiseView value, including source confusion and mis-centered SPHEREx. All totals count distinct active objects, independently of queue filters.
